\section*{The story in five steps}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Small model:} strongly anchored (index 0.82 vs about 0.49 for people). The anchor acted when the model recalled a value, not by gradual adjustment.
  \item \textbf{Large model:} it knew almost every answer, so it was hardly anchored (0.00 to 0.05). Where it was unsure, the anchor chose which memory won.
  \item \textbf{Screening round 1:} 206 new questions tested without anchors; 15 good Study~1 questions picked that the large model does not know.
  \item \textbf{Screening round 2:} asteroid orbits and 2026 events tried for Study~2; 10 questions picked where thinking moves the answer.
  \item \textbf{Study 2:} a given starting value pulls harder than the model's own. Thinking helped in both cases, so the strong human-based prediction was not supported.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{What has not been run yet}
Study~1 on the 15 new questions (Experiment 3's picks) has not been run. That is the next main step. After it: Study~2 again with higher thinking limits, and smaller models (Qwen3-14B, 8B, 4B) to see whether anchoring grows as the model knows less.
